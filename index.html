01 What's actually happening in July?

Every July, leaders from around the world meet at the United Nations in New York to check in on some big global promises — clean water, reliable power, safer cities, and fair partnerships between richer and poorer countries. This meeting is called the HLPF (just think "the big check-in").

This year, it runs July 7–15. Jamaica is one of the countries giving an official update on how it's doing — this is the third time we've done this.

Official updates can sound very polished. This page exists so what's actually happening in our communities — the parts that don't always make it into the official version — gets said too, in our own words.
